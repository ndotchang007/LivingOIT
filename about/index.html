Motivated by lived experience

LivingOIT is led by teens and young adults who have navigated oral immunotherapy firsthand. Meet the peers behind our resource library and community support.

Jonah Chang

Jonah Chang is a high school junior from Cupertino, CA. He did oral immunotherapy for wheat, dairy, and eggs over the course of six years.

Caleb Lin

Caleb Lin is a junior in college from Houston, TX. He did oral immunotherapy for wheat, dairy, cashews, peanuts, and shrimp over the course of high school.

Anya Shah

Anya Shah is a high school sophomore from Charlotte, NC. She did oral immunotherapy for peanut over the course of 3 and a half years.

Sophia Memon

Sophia Memon is a high school junior from Glenview, IL. She did oral immunotherapy for peanuts over the course of seven years.

Koby Kawano

Koby Kawano is a sophomore in high school, from the California Bay Area. He took OIT for over 5 years for allergies to peanuts, tree nuts, and dairy.

Parker Goodman

Parker Goodman is a sophomore in high school. He lives in St. Louis Missouri, and has done OIT for a year for pistachios and cashews.
